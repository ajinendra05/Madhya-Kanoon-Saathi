### GS-070: How many days does the PIO have to reply to my RTI?

```
The Central or State Public Information Officer must reply to an RTI request as expeditiously as possible and in any case within thirty days of receipt, but if the information concerns the life or liberty of a person, it shall be provided within forty-eight hours. A five-day period is added if the application is given to an Assistant Public Information Officer. E1, E3
“either provide the information on payment of such fee as may be prescribed or reject the request for any of the reasons specified in sections 8 and 9” [IN.RTI2005:s7:ss1]
“a period of five days shall be added in computing the period for response specified under sub-section (1) of section 7” [IN.RTI2005:s5:ss2]

Based on the sources cited above (official copies last checked: IN.RTI2005 2026-11-08).

This is legal information, not legal advice. For your own case, contact the District Legal Services Authority (DLSA); free legal services depend on eligibility under section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987. NALSA Helpline: 15100.
```
