## 1. Background facts (from official documents)

### A. The Code on Wages, 2019 and the repeal of the Minimum Wages Act, 1948
- The Code on Wages, 2019 (Act 29 of 2019) commenced on **21.11.2025** for ss.1–41, s.69 and others, by
  **S.O. 5322(E) dt 21.11.2025** (India Code consolidated text, p.5 footnote).
- **s.69(1)** repeals the Minimum Wages Act, 1948.
- **s.69(2)** says anything done under the repealed Acts, including any notification or appointment, "shall be
  deemed to have been done … under the corresponding provisions of this Code and shall be in force to the extent
  they are not contrary to the provisions of this Code", until repealed under the Code. **s.69(3)** applies
  s.6 of the General Clauses Act, 1897. (India Code PDF, p.30.)
- **s.7(1)–(2)**: a minimum wage may include a cost of living allowance. That allowance is computed "by such
  authority, as the appropriate Government may by notification, appoint". (pp.9–10.)
- **S.O. 2451(E) dt 12.05.2026** (Gazette of India) appoints the **Director General, Labour Bureau** under s.7(2),
  but only for establishments where the **Central Government** is the appropriate Government. It supersedes
  S.O. 186(E)–192(E) of 19.01.2017.

### B. The March 2024 base-rate revision (pre-Code) and its litigation
1. **Notification एफ 4(बी)1/2014/ए-16**, MP Rajpatra (Asadharan) **No. 68 dt 04.03.2024**, under MW Act
   s.5(1)(b), revised the basic minimum rates for the 67 scheduled employments **from 01.04.2024**.
2. It was challenged by the **MP Textile Mills Association** and others: **WP 10772/2024, 9401/2024,
   11921/2024, 12606/2024** (MP High Court, Indore Bench).
   - The petitioner association got an interim stay on **08.05.2024**.
   - After the stay order of **21.05.2024**, the Labour Commissioner's notification
     **6/11/अन्वे./पांच/2024/15817-16066 dt 24.05.2024** applied the older **2016 base** with VDA from
     01.04.2024 (unskilled ₹10,175/month).
3. The High Court **disposed of the petitions on 10.02.2025**. Paras 15–16, as quoted in the circular below:
   - the State is directed to start a fresh revision procedure under the 1948 Act for the employments added
     by notification of January 2025 (Textiles & Made-ups 36(A); Apparel 59(A); Footwear 73)
   - the petitioner association stays exempt from the 04.03.2024 notification
4. **Labour Commissioner circular 1/आठ/वेतन/भाग-3/2024/6891 dt 28.02.2025** (signed by the Labour Commissioner):
   - **para 03:** the 04.03.2024 rates are again effective, with VDA, for all notified employments **except**
     textiles, made-ups, apparel and footwear
   - **para 04:** those three employments get the **pre-revision rates + VDA** until the fresh revision
5. **Circular 4157-356 dt 18.02.2026**: payment of **arrears** of the revised rates from 01.04.2024.

### C. The post-repeal VDA notifications (issued after 21.11.2025)
- **6445-6650 dt 31.03.2026**: rates with VDA for **01.04.2026–30.09.2026**.
- **1/11/अन्वे./पांच/2024/22288-337 dt 30.09.2026**: rates with VDA for **01.10.2026–31.03.2027**
  (unskilled ₹12,575/month). Both say they are issued "under the Minimum Wages Act, 1948". The Sept 2026
  notification says the VDA rises because the average CPI went from 425 to 431.

### D. MP rules under the Code: drafts only
- **Draft** MP Code on Wages Rules, 2026: first draft dated 05.01.2026; **revised draft No. 83/2026/A-16**
  (MP Rajpatra 30.04.2026), proposed under Code s.67(1), to supersede the Minimum Wages (MP) Rules 1958.
  - Proposed r.5 computes the cost of living allowance before 1 April and 1 October each year on CPI-IW.
  - Proposed r.4 creates an **advisory** Technical Committee chaired by the Labour Commissioner.
- Objection and action-taken reports are dated **24–25.06.2026**. **We found no final rules and no MP notification
  appointing a s.7(2) authority.**

## 2. Our current working position (to be confirmed or corrected)
- The Code governs. Pre-21.11.2025 notifications may continue under s.69(2) if not contrary to the Code.
- Post-21.11.2025 notifications that cite the repealed Act do not automatically get s.69(2) protection.
  A continuity argument may exist via the saved CPI-linked structure and s.7(1)–(2).

## 3. Questions

**On the 2024 base rates (L8)**
1. Is notification No. 68 dt 04.03.2024 (as reinstated by circular 6891 after the HC order of 10.02.2025)
   **saved by s.69(2)** of the Code, as "not contrary to the provisions of this Code"?
2. For **textiles/made-ups, apparel and footwear**: was the fresh revision directed by the High Court completed?
   If not, which rates legally apply to those employments today?
3. Does the High Court's order of 10.02.2025 (or any appeal from it) affect anything beyond what circular 6891
   says?

**On the post-repeal VDA notifications (L7)**
4. **Authority:** after 21.11.2025, who is the competent authority in MP to compute the cost of living allowance
   under **s.7(2)**? Is the old MW Act arrangement (Labour Commissioner computing VDA) saved by s.69(2) as an
   "appointment … or direction", or is a fresh MP notification under s.7(2) required?
5. **Power:** can a VDA revision issued in **2026** under the *repealed* MW Act be read as an exercise of power
   under the Code (s.69(2) "deemed … under the corresponding provisions"), or is it only valid as the continued
   operation of the *saved* 2024 base notification's CPI formula?
6. **Validity:** are the notifications of 31.03.2026 and 30.09.2026 legally enforceable today? If yes, on what
   basis; if not, what amounts apply?
7. **Draft rules:** does the April 2026 draft (proposed r.5) have any legal effect before it is finalised?

**On how we may speak to users**
8. Until these are settled, is it accurate to tell users: "The Labour Commissioner, MP has notified ₹X for
   01.10.2026–31.03.2027; its legal basis after the Code on Wages is under review; for a dispute contact the
   Labour Office or DLSA"? Or should we not state amounts at all?
